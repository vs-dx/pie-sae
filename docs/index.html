02 / Representation quality

Reconstruction across fourteen blocks

Every fitted dictionary uses 3,072 features and TopK = 32. Performance varies by block; high reconstruction alone does not demonstrate interpretability or a biological mechanism.

What reconstruction does—and does not—show

The dictionaries capture substantial information from sampled PIE activations under the selected sparsity budget.

  • Encoder/processor vectors correspond to latent positions; decoder vectors correspond to output genes.
  • Decoder features may reflect stable output-gene identity rather than perturbation-specific responses.
  • Independent refits of the same block have not established feature stability across training seeds.

The original model and SAE weights were fixed during the later analyses. The underlying HDF5 arrays and checkpoints are not distributed in the public repository.

04 / Computational tests

Changing model states is not the same as explaining biology

Notebook 07 subtracts selected decoded SAE components from PIE hidden states and measures model responses. The intervention does not force exact zero upon SAE re-encoding.

Feature 189 · decoder.0

Model outputs respond to ablation

32 / 32 selected conditions

Nonzero changes were observed across the three output heads. Mean changes were 1.21–1.36× those of five fixed norm-matched random directions, but the feature exceeded the mean control in only 17–19 of 32 conditions, depending on the head.

The result shows computational sensitivity to a nonzero hidden-state change, not unique biological or SAE-feature specificity.

Patching · feature 189

Donor transfer was not reliable

2–3 / 9 pairs improved per output head

In nine directed donor–recipient comparisons, patching moved predictions closer to the donor in only 3/9 (DE), 2/9 (LFC) and 3/9 (delta-p) pairs.

Alternative-direction, decoy-donor and reverse-patching comparisons did not establish reliable donor-state transfer. This negative result is part of the evidence.

Cross-layer response · decoder.0 → decoder.1

Geometrically matched pair: feature 189 → feature 413

In six selected validation conditions, ablating upstream feature 189 made downstream feature 413 the largest mean-absolute SAE activation responder out of 3,072 decoder.1 features, measured only on source-feature-active gene positions. The pair's signed decoder-direction cosine was approximately 0.3023.

Interpretation boundary. Feature 189 was more geometrically aligned with downstream feature 413 than any of sixteen tested learned-direction controls. The controls were not geometry-matched; this does not establish unique causal mediation through feature 413 or a biological causal pathway.

05 / Evidence boundaries

What the evidence can support

We separate observed associations, changes to the model's computation, and experimentally validated biological mechanisms.

Observed · conditional evidenceAssociations and correspondences

Feature–gene enrichment and adjacent-layer matching under defined statistical and permutation procedures.

Observed · conditional evidenceComputational sensitivity

Prediction and downstream-state changes induced by selected interventions in a fixed pretrained model.

Not establishedBiological mechanism or unique mediation

No wet-lab causal validation; the 189–413 result lacks geometry-matched mediation controls.

Other important limitations: previously selected conditions and features, gene-position dependence within conditions, overlapping ontology terms, finite permutation budgets, absence of repeated same-layer SAE refits, and no independent clean-environment replication of the full research pipeline. The saved 06b and 07 notebooks report 19/19 and 15/15 internal consistency checks; these are not independent methodological reviews.
